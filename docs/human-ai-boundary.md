# Human–AI Boundary

The Skill can inspect, organize, challenge, and expose gaps in supplied material. It can propose research actions and alternative exploration paths.

The designer remains responsible for:

- confirming evidence and correcting interpretations;
- deciding which research to conduct;
- choosing among design paths;
- deciding when a hypothesis is safe to prototype or implement;
- setting the final scope and claims.

The Skill must never turn a management statement, feature list, unsupported persona, or design proposal into verified user evidence. When evidence is missing, the correct output is a bounded gap and a next step—not invented research.
